# Design system — Link Registry

## Overview

A routing office, not a SaaS dashboard. The page treats short links as local registry entries: a large destination form, a dark issuing sheet, then a ruled register.

## Colors

- Paper: #EEE9DD
- Ink: #1E2834
- Slate blue: #4D7891
- Registry red: #C75D3D
- Issuing sheet: #202C39
- Warm action: #E4AC63

## Typography

- Display: Iowan Old Style / Palatino Linotype / Georgia, large and editorial.
- UI and route codes: Avenir Next for prose, Courier New for labels, dates, and generated codes.
- Uppercase labels are sparse and tracked; they are office notation, not decoration.

## Layout

- Wide paper margin with a compact masthead.
- Oversized two-line hero and a rotated circular local stamp.
- The creation form is a single dark horizontal issuing sheet.
- Registry entries are ruled rows, never cards.

## Elevation & Depth

No shadows. Contrast comes from the dark sheet, hairlines, and the circular stamp.

## Shapes

Square controls and one circular stamp. No rounded cards.

## Components

- Link header: registry name, scope, and demo boundary.
- Issue sheet: destination field, action, validation message, and local-state note.
- Route row: sequence, short code, destination, date, copy, and remove.
- Empty register: a quiet ruled state that explains the next action.

## Do’s and Don’ts

Do keep the redirect limitation visible. Do let URLs read like registry data.
Don’t imply real analytics, accounts, redirects, or security guarantees.
